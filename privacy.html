Privacy and cookie policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Who we are

This website belongs to Deepak Watch Center, Bus Park Road, Dhangadhi, Kailali, Sudurpashchim Province 10900, Nepal. You can contact us about privacy on 984-8420742 or 091-525060.

What we collect

This website does not collect personal information. It has no contact form, no account sign-up, no newsletter and no online shop.

If you call us, we use your number only to answer you. We do not sell or share it.

Cookies

This website does not set any cookies and does not use analytics or advertising trackers. Because of this, we do not show a cookie banner.

Google Maps

The map on our home page is provided by Google. It does not load until you press "Show map". When you do, your browser connects to Google, which may collect information such as your IP address and set its own cookies, under Google's privacy policy (opens in a new tab). The "Get directions" button opens Google Maps directly.

Hosting and server logs

Like every website, our hosting provider receives basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested. This is used to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. We do not use it to identify you.

Fonts

Our fonts are stored on our own server, so loading the site does not contact Google Fonts or other font services.

Your rights

Under Nepal's Individual Privacy Act, 2075 (2018), you can ask us what information we hold about you and ask us to correct or delete it. Contact us using the details above.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
